How to request deletion of your data

SocialManager Stats is a private tool run by MilanLUXArt. It does not create user accounts and does not keep a profile of you. The only personal data it may hold is your name or username and the text of a comment or message you sent to the Instagram account or the Facebook Page "Milan Lux Art".

To have that data deleted

  1. Send a message to the Facebook Page Milan Lux Art using Messenger.
  2. Write "Delete my data" and mention your name or username, so we can find your comments or messages.
  3. We delete the matching data from the tool and reply to confirm.

Remove the app's access

If you gave this app access to your own Facebook account, you can remove it at any time: Facebook Settings → Apps and websites (or Business Integrations) → select "SocialManager Stats" → Remove.

Privacy Policy

Come chiedere la cancellazione dei tuoi dati (italiano)

Scrivi su Messenger alla Pagina Facebook Milan Lux Art la frase «Cancella i miei dati» indicando il tuo nome o username: cancelliamo i tuoi commenti o messaggi dallo strumento e ti confermiamo. Se hai dato accesso all'app al tuo account Facebook, puoi toglierlo da Impostazioni → App e siti web (o Integrazioni business) → «SocialManager Stats» → Rimuovi.
