Privacy Policy

App: SocialManager Stats  ·  Operator: MilanLUXArt  ·  Last updated: 23 November 2026

What this app is

SocialManager Stats is a private tool used only by its operator, MilanLUXArt, to manage the operator's own Instagram account and the Facebook Page "Milan Lux Art": publishing posts, reading statistics and reading and answering comments. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data the app accesses

  • Content, statistics, comments and (when enabled) messages of the operator's own Instagram account and Facebook Page.
  • The name or username and the text of people who comment on or write to those accounts, only so the operator can read and reply to them.

The app does not ask visitors to log in and does not collect data from anyone else.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is stored on the operator's own computer and is used only to run the tool described above.
  • To help write reply drafts, the text of a comment may be sent to a third-party AI service. The operator reviews replies before they are relied on.
  • We do not sell personal data and do not share it for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Comments and messages are kept only as long as needed to manage the accounts and can be deleted on request. See how to request deletion of your data.

Contact

Write to the Facebook Page Milan Lux Art using Messenger.

Informativa sulla privacy (italiano)

L'app legge solo i contenuti, le statistiche, i commenti e (se attivi) i messaggi di quegli account, incluso il nome o username e il testo di chi commenta, al solo scopo di permettere al gestore di leggere e rispondere. I dati restano sul computer del gestore; per preparare bozze di risposta il testo di un commento può essere inviato a un servizio di intelligenza artificiale di terzi. Non vendiamo i dati e non li usiamo per pubblicità.

Puoi chiedere la cancellazione dei tuoi dati scrivendo alla Pagina Facebook Milan Lux Art su Messenger; vedi anche come chiedere la cancellazione.
